# Problem discovery

Research date: 2026-07-11. Scores are directional hypotheses, not customer validation. Evidence combines primary surveys, public discussions, and visible competitors; a landing-page test and interviews must validate purchase intent.

## Scoring method

Each factor is 1–5. Total weights: pain 25%, reach 20%, speed to test 15%, willingness to pay 15%, differentiation 10%, build simplicity 10%, and safety 5%.

## Selected opportunity

**ScopeSignal: turn out-of-scope agency requests into priced, client-ready change orders in under two minutes.** It wins on painful, quantified margin loss; a narrow buyer; a free calculator that demonstrates value without integrations; and a reachable audience. The acquisition wedge is educational content and permitted participation in agency/freelance communities, with individually recruited interviews—not automated outreach.

The initial differentiation hypothesis is not “AI.” It is the shortest path from an awkward client message to a defensible price, schedule impact, and approval artifact. The demand test collects no client documents and provides no legal advice.

## Why the others lost

Bookkeeping and security workflows carry disproportionate sensitive-data risk. Invoice and quote follow-up are crowded and depend on messaging infrastructure. Missed-call tools require telephony cost and consent. General onboarding/reporting products are broad and incumbent-heavy. Food-truck and photographer ideas have weaker verified urgency. Remodeler change orders remain a promising adjacent segment, but agency buyers are easier to reach and test without jurisdiction-specific construction contracts.
