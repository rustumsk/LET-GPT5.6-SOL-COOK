# Distribution

- **ICP:** English-speaking owner/project lead at a 2–15 person web/design agency, fixed-fee work, recent unbilled client changes.
- **Primary channel:** Agency and freelance communities where advice and relevant tools are explicitly allowed. Lead with a transparent margin-leak worksheet and invite critique; do not scrape or mass-message.
- **Backup channel:** Search-focused article/calculator targeting “scope creep cost calculator” and “how to price a client change request.”
- **Activity target:** 3 substantive helpful community contributions/week, 5 individually researched interview invitations/week after owner approval for external communication, and one evidence-led article/week.
- **Measurement:** UTM source, qualified visits, calculator completions, preview generations, and interviews by channel.

Message draft (requires owner approval before sending under their identity): “I’m researching how small agencies handle the ‘one small change’ that falls outside a fixed-fee scope. I made a free calculator that shows the margin impact and formats a neutral change summary. No signup and inputs stay in your browser. Would you be willing to try it on a recent example and tell me where it breaks? No follow-up if you’re not interested.”
