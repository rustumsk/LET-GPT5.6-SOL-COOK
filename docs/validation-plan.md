# Validation plan

- **Target user:** Owner or project lead at a 2–15 person English-speaking web/design agency doing fixed-fee client projects.
- **Problem hypothesis:** Small out-of-scope requests are accepted informally because pricing and presenting a change order feels slower and more awkward than absorbing the work.
- **Value proposition:** See the real margin leak and create a calm, client-ready change-order summary in under two minutes.
- **Core action:** Complete the calculator and generate/copy a change-order preview.
- **Primary acquisition:** Helpful, non-promotional scope-management posts in agency/freelance communities where allowed, leading to the free calculator.
- **Success threshold:** In 14 days, 100 qualified visitors, 20 calculator completions, 8 preview generations, and 5 qualified interview/waitlist submissions; at least 3 report a recent real incident and agree to test the concierge workflow.
- **Failure threshold:** Fewer than 3 qualified leads or fewer than 10% of qualified visitors finish the calculator after 100 qualified visits.
- **Test duration:** 14 days after a public URL and approved channel activity exist.
- **Only after validation:** Accounts, saved projects, client approval links, email delivery, payments, integrations, and AI extraction.

The initial asset stores no input on a server. A waitlist is deferred until an approved privacy notice and data endpoint exist; the CTA uses a user-initiated email draft during the earliest concierge test.
